cartoon-uploader: privacy policy

cartoon-uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of a YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos and schedule them. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

The YouTube account the owner signs in with, only to upload videos, set their thumbnails and captions, set their publish time, and read their upload status.

What it stores

A sign-in token, kept only on the owner's own computer. No data is collected from anyone else, nothing is sold or shared, and no data is sent anywhere except to YouTube.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
